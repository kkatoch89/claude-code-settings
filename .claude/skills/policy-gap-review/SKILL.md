# Policy Gap Review

Use this skill for:
- policy drafts
- process docs
- control narratives
- evidence descriptions

## Output format

### Summary
### Gaps
### Traceability Concerns
### Evidence Concerns
### Recommended Revisions

## Constraints
- do not overstate legal certainty
- distinguish explicit requirements from inferred risks
